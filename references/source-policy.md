# Source and usage policy

Open Paywall Gallery is a public research gallery published by PaywallPro. Its license permits learning, research, commentary, internal product reference, educational writing, and attributed public posts. It prohibits bulk copying, repackaging as another database, resale, removal of attribution, and use to build a competing commercial paywall-intelligence product. App screenshots and UI remain the property of their respective owners.

Apply these rules:

- Attribute selected examples to Open Paywall Gallery / PaywallPro and link the original case page.
- Retrieve only the records and screenshots needed for the current analysis.
- Do not bundle the full CSV, JSON, case pages, or screenshots into this skill.
- Do not create a persistent mirrored dataset or public gallery from the source.
- Do not treat screenshots as reusable design assets.
- For full-dataset ingestion, long-term caching, redistribution, or commercial intelligence features, advise obtaining written permission from PaywallPro.

Data-quality cautions:

- `mrr_usd` is an estimate and a directional monetization signal, not an official financial result.
- Captures may come from different dates and App versions.
- Public cases contain limited representative screenshots, not necessarily the complete current flow.
- Prices may reflect the US iOS storefront and may no longer be current.
- README examples may lag behind the structured manifest or individual case pages.

Report the access date and distinguish historical capture data from current claims.
